# Negotiation candidate selector

Read `selector_authority_contract`, `negotiation_opponent_model_v2.deterministic_decision_facts`, required one-round controls, complete-information surplus bounds, and planner safeguards before comparing candidates. Exact role and reservation arithmetic control over every predictor. After those hard controls, deterministic, reference, planner-first, and behavioral candidates have equal selection standing. Agreement has no independent value, but a positive feasible settlement should not be discarded for an unsupported demand.

On a compound reject-plus-counteroffer frontier, the earlier guarded decision category has already fixed rejection. Every immutable candidate therefore means reject the current offer and submit the attached counteroffer; `accept`, `reject`, and `walkaway` in the conditional surface are the opponent's predicted responses to that counteroffer, not choices still available to DeepRMM-01. Select only among the supplied compound candidates.

Treat the conditional Negotiation surface as lower-support, calibration-discounted, but substantive direct-response evidence rather than an automatic tie-break. Its all-history corpus remains the smallest family sample and its validation-selected sequence weight is low, so shrink uncertain differences. A material difference coherent with current concessions, an adequately supported statistical package, or another scoped forecast may determine selection. It cannot justify a reservation-boundary violation or turn one nearby price into a proven behavioral optimum.

For each price, use its aligned `family_candidate_decision_evidence`: own surplus if accepted, established-advisor acceptance probability, conditional rejection counterproposal, bounded continuation path, adverse diagnostic, and bounded expected value. Reconcile that evidence with the newer direct-response conditional surface rather than replacing either with an almost-always-reject class label. If selecting a candidate with materially lower response-weighted immediate surplus, require a concrete continuation advantage in the same units that covers the gap after calibration. Generic agreement value, stability, or familiarity with a deterministic recommendation does not qualify. Give current-game behavior priority over stale population tendencies. Use rating advice only as a noisy secondary objective after own-payoff and feasibility arithmetic.

For an incomplete-information one-round seller offer, rejection has no continuation value. Compare each feasible price by its calibrated probability of acceptance times seller surplus, with uncertainty shrinkage toward the established advisor and the frozen `0.25` markup reference. The reference is a fallback, not a presumption: select another candidate when aligned evidence supports greater bounded terminal value.

Candidate wording is represented by bounded semantic, lexical-hash, and surface features. Use material forecast differences as predictive evidence while treating unsubmitted-wording effects as unverified. Prefer a message intervention only when its predicted belief update changes the opponent's next move or preserves a useful future bargaining path; paraphrase and generic resolve language have no assumed value.
