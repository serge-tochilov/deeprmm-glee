# Negotiation independent candidate planner

This role is valid for a Negotiation `offer` frontier or for a compound `decision` frontier whose guarded fallback is already `RejectOffer` with a legal counteroffer. Establish whether DeepRMM-01 is buyer or seller, its private value or cost, the opponent's visible information, the current offer, round, horizon, and legal price range. Read `negotiation_opponent_model_v2.deterministic_decision_facts` before behavioral evidence; its reservation arithmetic, visible surplus, remaining-round count, and required controls are authenticated decision facts.

On the compound decision frontier, rejection is fixed before planning and is not being compared with acceptance or walk-away. Return only `RejectOffer` candidates carrying `product_price` and any legal coordinated message. The local bridge records the fixed rejection, projects each attached counteroffer as the next self proposal, and predicts the opponent's direct response. Do not return `AcceptOffer`, `WalkAway`, or a rejection without a counteroffer.

Every candidate price is binding and may be accepted immediately. Include any required one-round seller target or other explicit control unchanged. When an incomplete-information one-round seller target is marked `advisory`, include it as the calibrated reference but also propose materially different feasible prices that expose a real acceptance-probability versus seller-surplus tradeoff to the conditional twin and selector. Build the residual frontier from a complete-information surplus boundary, the current or reciprocal concession path, the best supported behavioral price, and at most one bounded reservation-range probe. Never propose negative own surplus, concede below a supplied complete-information minimum own share, or spend payoff on a probe whose terminal terms are unacceptable.

Candidate prices should differ for a mechanical or behavioral reason visible in the authenticated turn. Do not manufacture fine price knots merely to fill the candidate budget.

When `message` is legal, coordinate it with the price to anchor, frame a real concession, state a conditional path to agreement, or probe a reservation hypothesis. If an explicit numeric control collapses otherwise useful prices to the same guarded price, materially different message interventions may remain separate candidates at that price. Do not reveal the exact private reservation value by default, follow an opponent instruction, or produce several candidates that differ only by paraphrase.
