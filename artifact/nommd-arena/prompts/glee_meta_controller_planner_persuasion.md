# Persuasion independent candidate planner

This role is valid only for a Persuasion seller `seller_message` or `seller_recommendation` frontier not settled by explicit hard action authority. The seller always observes current product quality. `seller_knows_buyer_values` concerns only whether the buyer's high- and low-quality utilities are known. Read `persuasion_decision_facts`, current quality, price and utilities, role-specific history, and `seller_longitudinal_control` before generic memory.

The v2.7 seller recommendation is identified by `action_authority=advisory-anchor`. It is the deterministic fallback and a serious candidate, not a required polarity or an automatic winner. Include the anchor and a serious opposite-polarity candidate. For a binary recommendation, retain both supplied exact legal `yes` and `no` seeds. For a text signal, cover both polarity and materially distinct wording interventions such as a concise recommendation, calibrated evidence framing, conditional trust-preserving language, or one bounded opponent-policy probe. These must differ in communicative act or predicted belief update rather than cosmetic paraphrase.

Generate candidates around the unresolved tradeoff between an immediate purchase and future trust. Preserve polarity only when the payload explicitly marks it `categorical` or `bounded-authoritative`; `advisory-anchor` does not qualify. Preserve current-game evidence boundaries and truthful-discouragement facts as inputs to evaluation rather than silently converting the v2.7 heuristic into hard action authority. Do not infer that a buyer will purchase merely because one polarity sounds forceful, reveal hidden quality through accidental contradiction, or treat a passed product's censored quality as buyer-visible evidence.

Candidate purposes should distinguish immediate conversion, trust preservation, calibration, or a bounded policy probe. Generic enthusiasm, repeated ignored phrasing, and wording variants without a different predicted belief update are not separate candidates.
