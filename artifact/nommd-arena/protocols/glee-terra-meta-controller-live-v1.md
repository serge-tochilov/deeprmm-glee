## Live path

An eligible turn follows one serial decision path under the original server deadline: independent Terra High candidate planning, one local batched candidate-response forecast, and constrained Terra High candidate selection. Bargaining and Negotiation offers are eligible. A Negotiation decision is also eligible when its legal schema permits a counteroffer and the existing guarded baseline has already fixed the compound action category to `RejectOffer`; only the attached counteroffer and legal message then vary. Persuasion text seller messages and binary seller recommendations are eligible unless an explicit hard action authority settles them; the v2.7 seller recommendation is an advisory anchor and does not settle the turn. Other turns retain the existing one-call capacity-chain path.

The planner sees the authenticated worker payload, existing deterministic controls, established family advisors, statistical packages, account hypotheses, rating advice, and retrieved memory. It sees no output or model card from the newly activated conditional twin. It must return 2–5 exact legal candidates without ranking or selecting them.

Every planner candidate is normalized, passed through the existing family safeguards, deduplicated after guarding, reindexed, hashed, and frozen. Candidate identity covers the complete legal action, including a legal message: numeric guards preserve wording so same-number candidates remain distinct when they are materially different messaging interventions. Bargaining and Negotiation insert the guarded deterministic fallback as a required candidate and stable comparison reference. Persuasion inserts the v2.7 seller anchor plus the complete opposite-polarity alternative for both binary and text seller frontiers, so the twin always evaluates both semantic choices. Required seeds occupy the first frozen positions and the bounded set retains at most 5 unique actions. The credential-free local sidecar warms all 3 frozen family heads before publishing readiness, then receives the exact candidate hashes and actions plus the bounded synthetic feature bundle, projects predictor-equivalent actions once, evaluates all unique projections in one serialized GPU batch, and restores the original candidate order. It emits aligned direct-response probabilities and no action.

The selector receives the authenticated turn, immutable candidate set, aligned conditional surface, frozen family model card, and machine-readable symmetric authority contract. It may return only one supplied candidate index and cannot emit a cognitive update. The selected action is resolved locally from the frozen set; selector-generated action text is structurally impossible.

## Canonical tetrad-free and position-deconfounded cutover

The canonical roles preserve a concise common evidence hierarchy and planner-stage commitment contract while supplying detailed family planner policies and full selector-stage and family-selector guidance. Planning therefore receives enough family structure to generate a serious bounded frontier without duplicated general prose, while selection receives the calibration, continuation-value, uncertainty, wording, and family-gate guidance needed to compare conditional forecasts. The process-local transport resolves each established planner or selector role to one fixed 3-part composition and records the effective role plus prompt digest in every model-call receipt.

The operational payload is tetrad-free before either Terra call: no activated ABDE records are retrieved or serialized, the planner returns only immutable candidates, the selector returns only an index, and the worker commits no model-authored cognitive update. Append-only game events, exact actions, statistical packages, account evidence, tactics, and run receipts remain available because they are behavioral evidence rather than a simulated private cognitive state.

Canonical candidate order remains fixed for mechanics, guards, hashes, family evidence, fallback identity, replay, and submission. The selector alone sees a seed-stable permutation committed from the authenticated turn ID and immutable candidate-set hash. Its presentation index is inverted to the canonical action before family policy runs, and each receipt preserves both mappings. This deconfounds position from deterministic-reference and planner-order semantics without randomizing the underlying decision frontier.

## Family-specific selector policy v1

The common symmetric selector remains unchanged, but a local family policy validates its exact selected index before submission. The policy is recorded under contract `glee-family-selector-policy-v1`; failure is closed to the already guarded deterministic or longitudinal fallback, not to another cloud call.

Bargaining distinguishes material allocation from message realization. A changed numeric allocation is authorized only when its expected-value gain over the required deterministic reference clears a blended conditional-twin margin, a separate sequence-model margin, and non-regression checks against the engineered component and established v2.17 behavioral advisor. If it fails, the reference allocation is restored while the selector's legal message is preserved, leaving wording-only interventions unrestricted.

Negotiation exposes the existing v2.9 candidate evaluator for every frozen price under `glee-family-candidate-decision-evidence-v1`. Each aligned row includes feasible own surplus, the established response forecast, the conditional rejection counterproposal distribution, central bounded continuation path, separate adverse diagnostic, and bounded expected value. Terra reconciles that candidate-specific evidence with the newer direct-response twin instead of reasoning from an almost-always-reject class label; no new post-selector numeric authority is introduced.

Negotiation arithmetic is role-explicit in the tetrad-free roles: seller own surplus is price minus seller cost, buyer own surplus is buyer value minus price, and a candidate-conditioned `accept` label means the opponent accepts our candidate price. The generic fallback and deterministic guard no longer accept zero own surplus or emit an exact reservation-value counteroffer when a positive-surplus price is feasible. The richer v2.9 advisor remains authoritative when available. Offer turns enter the ordinary 1.5-round frontier. On a guarded reject-plus-counteroffer decision, the client records rejection as fixed context and projects only the attached counteroffers onto that same offer-response frontier; terminal acceptance, walk-away, final rejection, and any decision whose baseline category is not compound rejection retain the established path.

The Negotiation bridge changes no frozen predictor or sidecar runtime. It creates a copied in-distribution offer state whose event prefix appends the current opponent proposal and fixed self rejection, increments the round, and presents each counteroffer as the next self proposal. The sidecar validates and forecasts projected candidate hashes; the client validates those projected identities and restores the original immutable compound-action hashes before the selector sees the surface. One bridge receipt binds the source phase, projected phase, 2 causal bridge events, source and projected game hashes, source and projected candidate-set hashes, and the explicit per-candidate hash mapping.

Persuasion requires both the longitudinal anchor and the opposite polarity in the frozen set. Same-polarity wording remains selector-controlled. An opposite polarity must improve blended purchase probability by at least `0.12 + 0.08 × remaining-horizon-fraction` and improve sequence-model purchase probability by at least 75% of that margin; otherwise the anchor polarity is restored. A final-round binary seller recommendation has no wording or later-trust decision left, so the frozen v2.7 anchor bypasses both Terra calls.

The 3 policies retain distinct promoted family seeds and family-tagged advisor journals inside one run label. Their historical frontiers and cutover receipts remain separable by family even though v93 intentionally shares one process lifecycle, broker, API ledger, and worker pool.

## Symmetric selector authority

After legality, categorical or bounded-authoritative action controls, exact role and payoff arithmetic, required terminal actions, planner-candidate safeguards, and deadline constraints have been applied, every remaining guarded candidate has equal selection standing. Candidate index, planner order, purpose, deterministic or analytic origin, reference status, and advisory-anchor status confer no presumption. The deterministic action remains the safe execution fallback if staged inference fails, but during a successful selector call it is merely one candidate and receives no prior weight.

The selector compares candidates in common expected-payoff units under the family objective. Bargaining combines accepted own allocation with predicted direct response and bounded discounted continuation; Negotiation combines feasible own surplus with predicted direct response and remaining-round continuation; Persuasion combines current sale payoff with purchase probability and a bounded estimate of effects on later products. Choosing a candidate with materially lower response-weighted immediate value requires a concrete continuation benefit, bounded in the same payoff units, that covers the immediate gap after calibration and uncertainty. Generic trust, fairness, stability, familiarity, candidate-purpose text, and unquantified future value do not meet that burden; continuation effects shrink with the remaining horizon and are zero after a terminal response.

The contract increases Terra's authority over candidate choice, not the conditional twin's authority. The twin remains an advisory direct-response predictor, its family calibration still discounts uncertain differences, and its output cannot override hard controls or prove the causal effect of an unsubmitted action. Symmetry means that a material coherent forecast is not demoted merely because it conflicts with the deterministic fallback, while an uncertain or incoherent forecast is not promoted merely because it is learned.

## Deadline and fallback

The shared model-work window is 100 seconds for Bargaining and 108 seconds for Negotiation and Persuasion. The planner is capped at 48 seconds, the local client at 3 seconds, the selector must begin with at least 12 seconds remaining, and 12 seconds are reserved for finalization and submission. The selector receives all remaining model-window time after planning and local inference.

Planner failure, invalid candidates, fewer than 2 distinct guarded actions, conditional-sidecar unavailability, model or candidate hash mismatch, incomplete forecast coverage, selector failure, invalid selector index, or insufficient remaining time returns the existing guarded deterministic action. For a Persuasion seller turn this fallback is the v2.7 longitudinal-policy anchor, while explicit buyer action authority remains hard and may bypass cloud inference. Failure does not fall through to an unbounded third model call.

## Receipts and interpretation

Each selected or failed staged turn records the deterministic baseline, planner role and timeout, immutable candidate set and hashes, local conditional receipt and aligned surface when available, aligned family evidence when available, selector role and timeout, presentation-to-canonical position mapping, selected canonical candidate, family-policy disposition, submitted action, safeguards, transport issues, elapsed time, and fallback reason. Model-call metadata binds each configured role to the effective balanced role and exact prompt digest, while the canonical module specification binds the balanced command-line selection. The selector payload records the exact authority contract used for that decision. Persuasion submission receipts distinguish an advisory anchor that was selected from one deliberately overridden; the family-policy receipt then states whether the calibrated polarity margin authorized submission. The family run manifest records the controller, selector backend, family selector policy, and sidecar contracts and immutable activation receipt.

Candidate-conditioned differences are predictive evidence for the opponent's immediate response. They are not proof of a counterfactual causal effect for an unsubmitted candidate or wording choice, and they do not predict later continuation, rating effect, or payoff-optimality. Prospective family-stratified outcomes are required to evaluate decision value.
