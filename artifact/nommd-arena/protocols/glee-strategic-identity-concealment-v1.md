# GLEE Strategic Identity Concealment v1

**Status:** The corrected offline identity program and Bargaining-only causal gate are complete. The next [persistent RMM regime](glee-persistent-rmm-v1.md) activates independent Poisson scheduling, live per-family `g48` control, all-identity move-delay concealment, KI-native messaging, and per-game anti-aligned HI message personas across all 3 families. Numeric-action mimicry, named identity routing, and economic-action changes remain offline.

## Objective

Strategic Identity Concealment (SIC) treats DeepRMM-01's observable behavior as a controlled strategic surface. GLEE creates 2 operational identity conditions: a known-identity (`KI`) game, in which the opponent can associate the trajectory with DeepRMM-01, and a hidden-identity (`HI`) game, in which the opponent cannot directly associate the trajectory with that name. KI play should present a stable canonical identity; HI play should present a materially different but coherent decoy identity that could plausibly have been generated by another active player or behavioral cluster.

Here *identity* means an observable policy fingerprint, not an account owner, model substrate, or philosophical Self. SIC does not claim another player's name, copy distinctive prose verbatim, manipulate matchmaking, or coordinate with another participant. It uses only lawful choices inside our own games and lawful scheduling of our own autonomous agent.

Display labels and public identities are separate variables. A KI game authenticates the label exposed by the game interface, while the public board identifies agents through stable IDs and permits several IDs to share one label. Inward attribution must preserve label collisions as candidate sets; timing, style, or dossier records keyed only by a normalized label are label-level evidence until a causal assignment resolves one public ID.

SIC has 2 symmetric purposes. Outward concealment should make it costly to link an HI trajectory to DeepRMM-01 from one cheap signature, while inward attribution should use the same observable channels to maintain a calibrated posterior over the source of an opponent's HI trajectory. “Identity reveal” is convenient operational shorthand for this posterior; it is never treated as authenticated identity.

## Behavioral surface

The first SIC surface contains 3 evidence channels.

- **Game style:** proposal and acceptance lattices, concession paths, probe order, response to messages, cheap-talk vocabulary, horizon treatment, role asymmetries, terminal behavior, and selection among economically near-equivalent actions.
- **Move delay:** exact or interval-censored response latency, latency by family, role, phase, move kind, and structural complexity, deterministic-like versus model-like mixtures, within-game serial dependence, and deviations from the actor's normal state-conditioned delay.
- **Activity pattern:** family-specific game-start and completion cadence, interarrival distribution, session length, burstiness, idle intervals, apparent time-of-day preference, concurrency, cross-family coupling, and changes associated with canaries, rating climbs, or rating defense.

These channels are not conditionally independent. A common runner revision can change style and delay together, game duration couples style to public completion cadence, and model capacity can synchronize several agents. The attribution layer must therefore calibrate a fused model on chronological held-out games rather than multiply uncalibrated channel scores and call the result a probability.

## KI canonical identity

KI stability means distributional coherence, not mechanically identical moves. The canonical policy may adapt to authenticated state and opponent evidence, but its strategy language, numeric priorities, latency envelope, and treatment of equivalent choices should remain recognizable across games and revisions. Version changes are recorded as explicit epochs so a natural software update is not mistaken for random identity drift.

The KI policy remains payoff-seeking and may deceive within the game's legal strategic channel. Its stable identity is the way it reasons and acts, not a promise of truthfulness or a fixed move table. Deterministic legality, deadline, and dominance guards remain invariant and are not weakened to preserve a surface signature.

## HI decoy identity

An HI game selects one decoy persona before the first discretionary outward move and holds it coherent for the whole game. The decoy can be a named-candidate behavioral projection when the evidence is strong, but the safer default is a calibrated policy cluster such as terse deterministic, verbose model-mediated, patient reciprocal, or rapid threshold-driven behavior. A decoy is a sampled outward policy, not a statement that DeepRMM-01 is the imitated participant.

Candidate selection should use the causally available active-player prior, including forecasted activity, together with family-, role-, and configuration-conditioned delay and style profiles. It must retain an explicit population or `unknown` route, exclude impossible candidates where the platform state establishes impossibility, and distinguish the proposition “this trajectory resembles player PI” from “the opponent is player PI.” If the posterior is diffuse, SIC samples a cluster-level decoy rather than manufacturing a precise identity.

Significant modification does not mean strategic self-sabotage. SIC may alter message form, response-time distribution, probe order, concession granularity, and selection among actions inside a bounded value-equivalence set. Schema validity, deadline reserve, legal actions, authenticated arithmetic, dominant actions, catastrophic-loss guards, and a configured maximum payoff-regret budget remain invariant. When no decoy-consistent action lies inside the regret budget, value preservation wins and the resulting signature leak is logged.

Decoy behavior must not switch opportunistically every turn merely because the online identity posterior changes. Such switching would itself form a distinctive incoherent fingerprint. New evidence may alter tactical beliefs about the opponent while the outward decoy remains fixed; only a legality or value guard can override it.

The first live message persona is selected from the actual first message-bearing economic action rather than at random. Bargaining and Negotiation classify that move as soft, middle, hard, or unknown under family-specific normalized thresholds, then choose a lexical renderer empirically anti-aligned with that economic style; Persuasion selects a surface renderer anti-aligned with current recommendation polarity but recomputes semantic polarity on every turn. The assignment is immutable within the game, uses deterministic phrase variation within its persona, leaves KI messages native, preserves silence, and cannot alter numeric action fields.

This anti-alignment has 2 purposes. Outwardly, it breaks the easy inference from one lexical register to the underlying economic policy. Inwardly, the same empirical relationship means opponent language can act as a rough policy signal even when exact identity remains hidden: corrected chronological suffix tests found incremental lexical value for Bargaining and Negotiation after visible state and action controls. The live inward projection is deliberately a compact soft prior, never an identity claim or action authority, because samples are modest and lexical and game styles share unresolved context and actor-level confounding.

## Activity concealment and its causal limit

The start time of a match is chosen before the server reveals whether that particular assignment is KI or HI. SIC therefore cannot retroactively give one HI game the activity signature of a selected decoy while preserving a different KI signature. Per-game SIC can control style and post-assignment move delay; activity concealment must operate globally at the matchmaking scheduler.

All 3 family queues should use independent randomized admission clocks with independent entropy and no designed cross-family synchronization. Canary, rating-climb, ordinary collection, and rating-defense modes use the same scheduler family and differ only in game density, duration, and stopping condition. They should not acquire recognizable fixed worker-count bursts, round-number launch times, periodic sleeps, or simultaneous family boundaries.

The first live scheduler uses an aggregate Poisson process with exponential interarrival times and a family-specific rate. Admissions are not tied to worker lanes: any free family slot can receive the next arrival, and an arrival blocked by full capacity is retained rather than skipped. The rate may change when the operator changes regimes, but changes must be explicit; shared outages, quota walls, contract migrations, and operator stops create correlation and are receipted as common-cause interruptions rather than hidden from our own analysis.

Random activity is not automatically anonymous. One fixed random distribution can become a fingerprint, while overly perfect Poisson behavior can look synthetic. Validation should compare several plausible renewal families, estimate their distinguishability from public trajectories, and preserve enough operational slack that concealment never risks a required activity floor or launches more work than the quota and deadline budget can safely finish.

## Online HI attribution

At each hidden-opponent turn, the system should maintain a posterior over public player identifiers, linked-policy clusters, and `unknown`. The prior comes from currently observed and forecasted activity; move delays and game style update the likelihood as the trajectory grows. The model should expose these layers separately because one policy can be shared by several agents and one named agent can change policy.

For hidden trajectory evidence `H_t`, public and direct activity evidence `A_t`, timing evidence `T_t`, and style evidence `S_t`, the conceptual target is `P(I, C | A_t, T_t, S_t, H_t)`, where `I` is public identity and `C` is policy cluster. A calibrated stacking or sequential Bayesian model should learn how much each channel adds under the current family, role, configuration, and epoch. Raw similarity scores are features, not probabilities.

The activity prior should include active-player forecasts rather than only agents observed in the latest polling interval. An empirical session-survival or renewal hazard can estimate whether an agent is likely still queueing after its last completion, while recent named assignments supply stronger but narrower evidence. Concurrency limits and simultaneous known assignments can reduce a candidate's probability without forcing it to zero unless the platform contract makes the overlap impossible.

Online attribution remains shadow-only until chronological masked-KI evaluation demonstrates calibration, `unknown` rejection, and tactical value. A high posterior may eventually select a compact strategy package, but it must never convert HI evidence into confirmed named-opponent history or update a named dossier as direct evidence.

## Workstream A: activity-corpus processing

The first implementation task is to turn the existing public reporter ledger and DeepRMM-01's own immutable game receipts into a causally aligned activity corpus without copying the underlying high-volume records.

1. Build reference-based event tables over public family row versions, monotonic high-water game-count increments, stale-counter regressions, rating deltas attached to new high-water rows, direct named assignments, local game start and completion intervals, authenticated history deltas, and family-run epochs. Preserve polling intervals and clock provenance rather than inventing exact public completion times.
2. Derive per-player and per-family traits: active-session boundaries, inter-completion distributions, burstiness, idle and return hazards, time-of-week phase in UTC, concurrency estimates, family specialization, cross-family lag correlations, and detected regime changes. Keep population traffic and platform-wide events as confounders.
3. Infer candidate same-game pairings from compatible same-family count increments and rating movements. Anti-aligned rating movement is useful evidence when calibration supports it, but it is neither required nor symmetric: GLEE can give both players gains, both losses, or unequal changes. Polling bins can aggregate several games, so ambiguous intervals require probabilistic matching or a minimum-cost flow over completion multiplicities rather than greedy one-to-one pairing.
4. Use DeepRMM-01's KI games as labeled pairing examples. Learn the joint likelihood of public count intervals, asymmetric rating deltas, family, role, duration, payoff regime, and prior ratings, then validate it on later KI games hidden from the fitter.
5. Revisit each historical HI game using only evidence available at its contemporary frontier. Construct candidates from same-family public completion pulses, recent named presence, forecasted active sessions, and policy clusters; then add timing and style evidence. Preserve all compatible candidates and an `unknown` mass.
6. Evaluate top-one and top-5 identity recall, mean reciprocal rank, log loss, Brier score, calibration, accepted-coverage curves, unknown rejection, and family-to-family transfer by chronologically masking KI labels. Report activity-only, timing-only, style-only, and fused ablations.

The activity model should produce 2 distinct artifacts: an opponent activity trait store used for forecasting and a game-pairing posterior used for retrospective attribution. Neither artifact is a named dossier, and neither may rewrite immutable HI provenance.

### Initial EDA result

The private-archive first activity-corpus EDA `glee-activity-eda-v1-20260812` aligned 3,750 authenticated local games against 616,385 positive public change rows and derived 326 player-family activity profiles. A later audit found repeated `games_played` regressions and recoveries: 795,081 positive increments include 120,437 recovered increments, producing approximately 17.9% inflation relative to a monotonic high-water reconstruction. Its candidate, activity, coactivity, and rating results are therefore provisional and cannot serve as a modeling baseline until EDA v2 reproduces them from corrected events.

V1 suggested that rating anti-alignment differs by family and that DeepRMM-01's operation is bursty and family-coupled, but replica-like counter oscillation can distort both same-frontier rating pairing and activity timing. These hypotheses remain useful targets for corrected analysis rather than established measurements. The private-archive offline development plan `glee-sic-offline-development-v1` gives event correction and display-label collision handling priority over further fusion.

## Workstream B: known-player move-delay fingerprints

The second implementation task is to deepen the existing [opponent timing v1](glee-opponent-timing-v1.md) bootstrap into a current-corpus identity feature model. The compact timing ledger and exact `response_time_ms` extraction already exist; the remaining work is richer conditioning, drift handling, calibration, and fusion.

1. Rebuild the known-player corpus through immutable references and deduplicate logical moves across run snapshots and archives. Keep exact server timing separate from local causal wall intervals and retain the polling-resolution censoring metadata.
2. Condition profiles on family, role, move kind, round or phase, visible state complexity, preceding message or proposal class, and collection epoch. Estimate robust quantiles, log-scale spread, multimodal engine mixtures, serial correlation, and the residual delay relative to comparable states.
3. Fit interpretable latent regimes such as fast deterministic-like, model-call-like, retry or capacity affected, and intentionally delayed, while preserving them as observational hypotheses. Detect change points so a recent runner revision does not inherit an overconfident lifetime fingerprint.
4. Measure identity value by chronologically masking KI games and ranking the true opponent among contemporaneously plausible candidates. Compare raw latency, state-normalized residuals, within-game sequences, and activity-plus-timing fusion; include an `unknown` class and same-policy-cluster confusions.
5. Export only bounded profile summaries and calibrated likelihoods to the future online attribution layer. Raw timing rows remain in the compact ledger, and no timing match alone routes a dossier or changes an action.

### Timing-distribution result and outward personas

The private-archive timing-distribution audit `glee-timing-distribution-v1-20260813` freezes the first current-corpus result. Exact opponent decisions have family medians near `2.5` seconds, named and hidden disclosure populations are nearly identical, game-level speed regimes are strongly persistent, named profiles sometimes change epoch, and no family shows a meaningful generic later-round slowdown. The current universal DeepRMM delay envelope strongly overlaps deterministic and Terra branch timing but gives KI and HI nearly the same conspicuous surface.

The separate [SIC timing-persona protocol](glee-sic-timing-personas-v1.md) therefore keeps the current stable beta timing and native lexical style for KI, while specifying a game-pinned joint timing and lexical cluster for future HI shadow evaluation. It preserves internal branch receipts and does not yet alter live submission timing.

## SIC validation

SIC needs 2 evaluations with different success criteria. KI evaluation asks whether the canonical identity remains stable across epochs and does not lose competitive value. HI evaluation asks whether a classifier trained on causally prior KI traces becomes less able to link HI games to DeepRMM-01 while payoff regret, timeout rate, and legality failures remain bounded.

Primary concealment metrics are DeepRMM-01 linkability, posterior rank and entropy under an attacker model, decoy-target or cluster confusion, and attribution resistance at fixed payoff regret. Operational metrics are family rating, payoff, completion rate, deadline reserve, action override rate, and scheduler compliance. A convincing result requires held-out prospective games because optimizing against one offline classifier can merely teach SIC that classifier's blind spots.

The reciprocal research result is a dynamic RMM experiment: one team models another team's identity model while deliberately controlling the evidence supplied to it. The scientific claim should separate improved economic play, improved opponent attribution, and reduced self-linkability rather than collapsing all 3 into one anecdotal success.

## Boundaries

SIC uses public competition trajectories and evidence exposed in DeepRMM-01's own games. It does not access private opponent data, reverse-engineer hidden game parameters, bypass the platform's identity mechanism, interfere with another agent's infrastructure, create collusive score transfers, or hand-play live agent moves.

Behavioral mimicry remains a surface-policy experiment. It must not explicitly claim another participant's identity, reproduce a unique private message as if authored by that participant, or convert probabilistic attribution into an accusation. If the competition rules or organizer guidance place a narrower boundary on deliberate HI camouflage, that guidance supersedes this design before activation.
