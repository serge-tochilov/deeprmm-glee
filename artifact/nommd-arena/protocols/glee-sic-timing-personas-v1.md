# GLEE SIC timing personas v1

**Status:** Executable candidate frozen from the private-archive timing-distribution audit `glee-timing-distribution-v1-20260813`; not selected by the current pointer and not active in live runners

## Purpose

This protocol separates 2 outward timing objectives that the first universal delay envelope conflated. KI requires a stable recognizable DeepRMM identity while obscuring the internal deterministic-versus-model branch. HI requires a coherent but different cluster-level identity that resists linking to KI while preserving the same internal branch protection, economic policy, legality, and deadline safety.

Internal provenance is mandatory. Every move retains its `selection_branch`, model-call metadata, natural computation time, sampled target, applied delay, identity mode, timing-persona identifier, release reason, and deadline reserve. DeepRMM's surface may remain conspicuous while its internal deterministic-versus-model branch remains non-penetrable from that surface; concealment controls external observability and never deletes our branch ground truth.

## KI contract

The first KI epoch retains the existing bounded beta target on total move latency: minimum `14` seconds, maximum `46` seconds, shape `Beta(2,2)`, and the existing deadline-reserve release rule. KI messages remain native rather than passing through an HI renderer. This distribution and lexical register remain stable until an explicit KI epoch migration is documented and evaluated.

KI timing is sampled independently per move conditional on the same stable distribution. This preserves ordinary variation while avoiding deterministic timestamps. It is intentionally conspicuous as DeepRMM's public surface and is not intended to make KI resemble the population.

## HI assignment contract

One HI timing persona is assigned after the platform discloses hidden-opponent mode and before the first discretionary outward move. Its assignment is stored with the existing per-game lexical profile and remains immutable through the game. New opponent evidence may alter inward beliefs and economic play, but it cannot switch the outward timing persona.

The persona is a family-, role-, move-kind-, and collection-epoch-conditioned behavioral cluster, not a named identity. It contains a game-level log-speed distribution, phase residual distributions, feasible natural-latency support, lexical-persona compatibility, and a release identifier. Raw traces and exact named-player mimicry are excluded.

The proposed timing model is `log T_i = μ_{f,r,k,c} + b_g + ε_i`, where `c` is the pinned cluster, `b_g` is one game-level speed offset sampled at assignment, and `ε_i` is move-kind-conditioned residual noise. The model does not include a generic round-number trend because the frozen corpus found within-game round-delay correlations near zero. A future state-hardness term requires separate prospective evidence.

## Feasibility and branch concealment

The supervisor can delay a completed action but cannot accelerate natural computation. Persona selection must therefore reject any fast cluster whose support is incompatible with the possible live policy branches for that state and family. A fast persona may be selected when all applicable outward actions are guaranteed deterministic; mixed or slow personas cover states that may require cloud inference.

For each move, the supervisor samples a persona target and submits at `max(natural completion, sampled target)` subject to the existing deadline reserve. A deterministic branch receives enough delay to enter the persona distribution. If a model call naturally exceeds the sampled target, it passes through without additional delay and records a `natural-latency-override`; the persona does not switch mid-game.

Retries, capacity stalls, malformed responses, and deadline guards remain factual tail events. The supervisor does not issue a redundant model call, suppress a required retry, weaken a safeguard, or risk timeout merely to improve camouflage.

## Joint timing and lexical coherence

The timing assignment and HI lexical assignment share one immutable per-game profile. Their outward features should form a plausible cluster while remaining anti-aligned with DeepRMM's actual policy signature. Cluster selection may use whether the action path is guaranteed deterministic, but the rendered message cannot disclose that fact.

KI remains native in both channels. HI may use terse-fast, terse-mixed, model-mediated-slow, or other empirically supported clusters, but it must not copy a participant's distinctive private phrase, claim their identity, or change economic numeric fields to fit the persona.

## Executable release modes

Supervisor mode `glee-parallel-v32` accepts both `shadow` and `authoritative` release modes. Shadow mode computes and receipts the timing persona and counterfactual target while the current universal envelope remains authoritative. Authoritative mode uses the persona target after bounding it by the configured delay range and the existing deadline reserve. Both modes preserve KI and legacy assignments on the original beta law.

The release `v2-joint-hi-persona` was activated through the controlled transition recorded in the private-archive receipt `glee-persona-epoch-v1`, and `policies/message-style/current.json` points to that release. Continued evaluation reports KI-versus-HI DeepRMM linkability, deterministic-versus-model branch classification, cluster fit by family and move kind, within-game first-half versus second-half coherence, natural-latency overrides, deadline-reserve releases, timeouts, economic action equality, and payoff or rating regret. Numeric-action mimicry and identity-specific routing remain offline.

## Separate resource-exhaustion workstream

Long low-information Bargaining trajectories require a separate semantic-plateau control. A deterministic staircase, repeated intent, or tiny concession can keep a literal numeric plateau detector inactive while inducing repeated cloud calls. That defense may cache an unchanged economic response and bypass prose inference until a policy boundary changes, but it must be replayed and released independently from timing personas so compute savings are not mistaken for identity concealment evidence.
